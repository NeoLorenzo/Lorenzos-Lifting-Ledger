      How these numbers are calculated

      Sessions is a direct count of your dated workout-session records.

      Working sets counts set records that are not marked as warm-ups.

      Recorded volume adds weight in kilograms × reps for every working set where both values exist. It is a simple log total, not a measure of effort, fatigue, muscle growth, or work against gravity.

      The monthly charts show the latest 12 calendar months covered by your history, including months with no sessions. Top exercises are ranked by working-set count. Incomplete sets are retained in counts but excluded from volume.
